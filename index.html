Route instructions (optional)

Supported instructions: “first stops: 2, 5”, “north first”, “south first”, “north cutoff: 42”. Separate rules with semicolons. Default: reduce driving, no regional preference. North/south use a latitude boundary (40 by default), not a weather forecast. Unknown instructions block planning rather than being ignored. Individual First checkboxes also apply.
